Connecting experience with ambition

Your experience can change someone's future.

Share what you know. Shape what comes next.

BridgeForward is a proposed mentoring initiative connecting university students in STEM and business with volunteers who can offer real-world knowledge, practical guidance and encouragement.

Explore volunteering

A school project concept — not an operating mentoring service.

Our purpose

One conversation can open a door.

University students have big ambitions. Sometimes, what they need most is someone who has been there before.

BridgeForward aims to make professional insight more accessible, helping students bridge the gap between academic learning and the workplace.

Your time. Your experience. Their next step.

Mentoring opportunities

Two fields. Countless possibilities.

From technical problem-solving to entrepreneurial thinking, experience can help students explore new pathways.

STEM — Build the future

Support students exploring science, technology, engineering and mathematics.

  • Programming, data science and emerging technologies
  • Engineering and practical problem-solving
  • Scientific research and analytical thinking
  • Careers in technical industries

Business — Turn ideas into action

Help students connect business theory with professional practice.

  • Finance and accounting
  • Marketing and communications
  • Leadership, management and teamwork
  • Entrepreneurship and business planning
A proposed process

How BridgeForward could work

01

Connect

Students express their interests and mentoring goals.

02

Match

Volunteers and students are paired based on expertise and shared interests.

03

Grow

Mentors and students meet to discuss challenges, questions and career goals.

Mentoring could take place online or in person, depending on future programme arrangements.

Become a mentor

Give your experience a new purpose.

Professionals, graduates, entrepreneurs and others with relevant experience could make valuable mentors. Bring your knowledge, a willingness to listen and time for meaningful conversations.

Give an hour. Share a lesson. Help someone move forward.
